# Delete My Data

**Last updated: August 19, 2026**

## He Who Asks to Be Deleted Shall Be Deleted

You may request deletion of your **Reflections account and associated personal data** at any time.

No quest.  
No seven-step retention survey.  
No *“Are you really, really, really sure?”*

Just ask.

---

## How to Request Deletion

Use the **Delete Account** option in Reflections, if available, or contact:

**chat_app@crishub.com**

Tell us which Reflections account you want deleted. We may ask you to verify that the account is actually yours.

Because:

> **“Greetings. Please delete Kevin.”**

is insufficient authorization.

---

## When Will It Be Deleted?

As soon as reasonably possible after we receive and verify your request, and within any period required by applicable law.

Please give us a little time.

I may be:

**at the park,  
at the gym,  
sleeping,  
eating,  
at church,  
or staring at a production error wondering what I have done.**

Reflections is not operated by an enormous 24-hour privacy department.

But your request will be handled.

> **He who asks to be deleted shall be deleted...**
>
> **when I get back.**

---

## What Gets Deleted?

Subject to applicable legal and technical requirements, deletion includes personal information associated with your account that Reflections no longer has a legitimate reason or legal obligation to retain.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups, security records, or where retention is required by law.

Content that other people legitimately copied or saved while it was shared with them may also be outside our control.

---

## Facebook Login

If you used Facebook Login, requesting deletion from Reflections will also remove the Reflections account data subject to the conditions above.

Removing Reflections from Facebook does not necessarily delete information already stored by Reflections.

**If you want us to delete it, tell us.**

We will.

Eventually.

See previous section concerning parks, gyms, sleep, and the realities of a one-human privacy department.
